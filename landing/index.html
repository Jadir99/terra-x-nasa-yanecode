PLANETARY ANALOG SCIENCE

WHY STUDY EARTH'S EXTREME FRONTIERS?

Before sending rovers, drill rigs, and astronauts millions of kilometers into deep space, planetary scientists look to Earth. Terrestrial analog environments replicate the extreme aridity, abrasive basaltic regolith, radiation regimes, and geochemical signatures of extraterrestrial worlds.

Foundational Scientific Axiom

“A terrestrial analog reproduces specific relevant properties, never Mars or the Moon in their entirety.”

By benchmark-testing robotic instrumentation in hyperarid evaporite basins like Tindouf and Yungay, or sampling endolithic microbes in Antarctic dry valleys, we validate mission hardware, refine biosignature extraction protocols, and unlock the fundamental limits of extraterrestrial habitability.
